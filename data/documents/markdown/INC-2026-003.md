# INC-2026-003 — Kafka Partition Skew
## Root Cause
Partition-key distribution was highly skewed because a small number of customer identifiers generated most events.
## Impact
One consumer processed the hot partition while other consumers were underutilized.
## Resolution
The event keying strategy was changed after validating ordering requirements.
## Lesson
Increasing consumer count alone cannot solve the problem because partition ownership limits parallelism.
