INCIDENT EXPORT
Ticket: INC-2025-014
Title: Payment Incident — Duplicate Charge Spike
Severity: SEV-1
Status: Resolved

SUMMARY
A deployment caused duplicate payment authorization requests for a subset
of orders.

ROOT CAUSE
A retry loop did not preserve the Idempotency-Key across retries.

IMPACT
Duplicate authorization requests were observed for a subset of orders.

RESOLUTION
The faulty deployment was rolled back.
The retry client was changed to reuse the same idempotency key for the
entire logical request.

PREVENTION
The payment integration test suite now verifies idempotent retry behavior.
