BLUE-GREEN DEPLOYMENT GUIDE

MODEL
Two production environments exist: blue and green.
One serves live traffic while the other receives the candidate release.

PROCESS
Deploy the candidate to the inactive environment,
run health checks and smoke tests,
then switch traffic.

ROLLBACK
Rollback is a traffic switch back to the previously active environment,
provided database compatibility is maintained.

LIMITATION
Blue-green deployment does not remove the need for backward-compatible
database changes.
