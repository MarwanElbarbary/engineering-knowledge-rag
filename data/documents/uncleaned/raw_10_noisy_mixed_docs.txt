[API CATALOG]
GET /services lists registered services.
GET /services/{name} returns ownership, repository, on-call team, and criticality.
Authentication: OAuth 2.0 access tokens with catalog:read scope.

==============================

[OBSERVABILITY]
Production services must expose metrics, structured logs, and distributed traces.
Requests must propagate a correlation_id across service boundaries.
At minimum record request count, error count, latency, dependency failures.
