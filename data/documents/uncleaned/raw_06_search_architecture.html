Search Platform Architecture

Last updated: 2025-08-14

The search platform consists of an ingestion service, OpenSearch cluster, query API, and ranking service.

Retrieval

The first stage retrieves candidates using BM25. A second-stage learning-to-rank service may rerank the top 50 candidates.

Why Two Stages

Candidate retrieval must be inexpensive enough to operate at query volume. Reranking is more computationally expensive and is therefore applied to a smaller candidate set.
