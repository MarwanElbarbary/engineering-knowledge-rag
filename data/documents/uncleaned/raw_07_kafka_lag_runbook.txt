### KAFKA CONSUMER LAG RUNBOOK ###
EXPORT DATE: 2026/01/12
OWNER: PLATFORM

SYMPTOMS:
Consumer lag increases when consumers process records slower than producers publish them.

FIRST CHECKS:
- check consumer group lag
- check partition assignment
- check consumer error logs
- check downstream dependency latency

COMMON CAUSES:
1) Slow database operations
2) Insufficient consumer instances
3) Partition skew
4) Downstream service throttling

MITIGATION:
If partitions are available, scale consumers within the partition limit.
If the downstream dependency is the bottleneck, scaling consumers may make
the problem worse.

[NOTE FROM WIKI]
DO NOT interpret a temporary lag drop as full recovery.
Verify that lag decreases consistently.

### END ###
